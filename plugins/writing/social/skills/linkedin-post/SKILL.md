# LinkedIn post

Write one post of 120 to 200 words.

## Steps

1. Start with a line that makes the reader stop: a number or a surprise.
2. Tell one short story that supports the point.
3. End with a question to the reader.

## Rules

- Short paragraphs: one or two sentences.
- At most three hashtags, at the end.
