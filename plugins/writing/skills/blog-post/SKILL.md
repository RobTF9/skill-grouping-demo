# Blog post

Turn the outline into a post that a busy reader can scan in one minute.

## Structure

1. A title that states the main point.
2. One short paragraph that says why the reader should care.
3. Three to five sections, each with a heading that is a full sentence.
4. One concrete example for each section.
5. A last paragraph that says what to do next.

## Style

- Follow [the brand voice](references/voice.md).
- Use [the headline patterns](references/headlines.md) for the title.
- Keep paragraphs under four sentences.
- Use a list when you have three or more items.

## Length

Between 600 and 1,000 words. Cut a section before you cut an example.
