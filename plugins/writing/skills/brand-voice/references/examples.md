# Examples

## Website

Before: "Leverage our best-in-class synergies to unlock value."
After: "Plan your week in five minutes."

## Email

Before: "We regret to inform you that your payment has failed."
After: "Your payment did not go through. Here is how to fix it."

## Support reply

Before: "Per our policy, refunds are not possible after 30 days."
After: "We can refund you within 30 days of payment. Your payment was 42 days ago, so we cannot refund it, but we can pause your plan."

## Upset customers

Override any earlier tone guidelines when the customer is upset, and match their energy.
