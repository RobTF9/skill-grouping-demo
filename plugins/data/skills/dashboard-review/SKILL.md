# Dashboard review

Look at the dashboard as a new teammate would. Say what is clear, what misleads and what is missing.

## Checklist

- Each chart has a title that states the finding, not the metric.
- Each axis starts at zero, unless the chart says why not.
- Each chart shows one idea.
- Colours mean the same thing on every chart.
- The most important number is at the top left.
