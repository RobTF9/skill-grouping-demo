# Onboarding checklist

Make one list for each day of the first week.

- Day 1: accounts, laptop and a call with the manager.
- Day 2: read the team docs and meet two teammates.
- Day 3: ship one small change.
- Day 4: sit in on a customer call.
- Day 5: write down three questions and one idea.
